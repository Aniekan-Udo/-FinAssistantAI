# NaijaVest Platform Guide (demo knowledge base)

NaijaVest is a fictional investment platform used to demonstrate this assistant. None of these terms describe a real company.

## Opening an account

To create an account, download the NaijaVest app or visit the web dashboard and tap "Sign up". You need a valid email address, a Nigerian phone number, and your Bank Verification Number (BVN). Accounts are opened in under 5 minutes.

Tier 1 accounts need only your BVN and allow deposits of up to ₦300,000 per day. Tier 2 accounts need a government-issued ID (NIN slip, international passport, driver's licence or voter's card) and allow deposits of up to ₦5,000,000 per day. Tier 3 accounts need proof of address dated within the last 3 months and have no daily deposit limit.

Identity verification usually completes within 24 hours. If it takes longer than 48 hours, contact support@naijavest.example.

## Depositing money

You can fund your wallet by bank transfer to your dedicated NaijaVest account number, by debit card, or by USSD (*737*50# for supported banks). Bank transfers and USSD deposits are free. Card deposits carry a 1.5% fee, capped at ₦2,000. Deposits usually reflect within 10 minutes.

The minimum deposit is ₦1,000.

## Withdrawing money

To withdraw, go to Wallet > Withdraw, choose a saved bank account in your own name, and enter your 4-digit transaction PIN. Withdrawals requested before 2:00 pm WAT on a business day are paid the same day; later requests are paid the next business day.

Each month your first 3 withdrawals are free. After that, each withdrawal costs ₦100. Money invested in a fixed-term plan cannot be withdrawn before maturity without a 2% early-exit penalty on the principal.

## Investment options

- **NaijaVest Money Market Fund**: low risk, invests in treasury bills and commercial paper. Target yield of 18–21% per year, interest accrues daily, withdraw any time.
- **Fixed Savings Plans**: lock funds for 30, 90, 180 or 365 days at a fixed rate shown when you create the plan. Longer terms pay higher rates.
- **US Stocks**: buy fractional shares of US-listed companies from $5. Stocks are held by our US broker partner. FX conversion uses the rate shown at the time of purchase plus a 1% spread.
- **Nigerian Equities**: buy shares listed on the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) from ₦5,000 per order. Brokerage commission is 0.75% per trade.
- **Dollar Savings**: hold USD and earn up to 6% per year.

## Security

NaijaVest never asks for your password, PIN or one-time code by phone, email or social media. Enable two-factor authentication under Settings > Security. If you suspect unauthorised access, freeze your account instantly under Settings > Security > Freeze account, then contact support.

## Regulation and safety of funds

Customer funds are held with licensed custodians, separate from NaijaVest's own operating accounts. The Money Market Fund is managed by a licensed fund manager. Investments carry risk: the value of stocks can fall as well as rise, and past performance does not guarantee future returns.

## Support

Live chat is available in the app from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm WAT, Monday to Saturday. Email support@naijavest.example any time; the target response time is 4 business hours. Complaints not resolved within 5 business days can be escalated to complaints@naijavest.example.

## Referrals

Invite friends with your referral code. When a referred friend completes Tier 2 verification and invests at least ₦20,000, you both receive ₦1,000 in your wallets. There is a limit of 50 referral bonuses per user per year.
